Our Story

Brewing
Hope.

There is a saying in Uganda that a coffee tree can educate a child.

Our Impact Events

For generations, that belief has inspired families to nurture every coffee tree with hope. Yet for countless households, that promise has remained painfully out of reach.

In the steep mountains of Bududa, where fertile volcanic soils produce some of Uganda's finest Arabica coffee, countless families greet each dawn with both determination and uncertainty. A typical household depends entirely on a small coffee garden, lovingly tended and passed down through generations. Before the sun rises, parents make their way to the hillsides while children prepare for school, each day carrying the same quiet hope that this year's harvest will be enough to pay school fees, put food on the table, and create a future brighter than yesterday.

Yet life on these breathtaking slopes comes at a cost. During the rainy seasons, landslides threaten homes, gardens, livelihoods, and lives. Families who have cultivated these mountains for generations live with the constant uncertainty that one night of relentless rain could erase years of hard work.

Even when they survive nature's challenges, another battle begins. When the coffee cherries ripen, middlemen arrive and decide the prices. With no bargaining power, no storage facilities, limited access to quality processing, and little connection to international markets, farmers have little choice but to sell immediately to meet urgent household needs. Their coffee embarks on a journey across continents, growing in value with every step, while the hands that nurtured it remain among the least rewarded in the entire value chain.

This is not the story of one family. It is the story of thousands of coffee-growing households across Bududa, Mbale, Buikwe, Mukono, Luwero, and many other coffee-producing communities throughout Uganda.

For many children in the greater Mbale region, coffee represents both hope and uncertainty. Some walk long distances to school, uncertain whether they will complete the term because school fees depend entirely on the harvest. Others leave school too early to help in the gardens or seek work to support their families. They grow up surrounded by one of the world's most sought-after agricultural products, yet many never experience the opportunities that its global success should bring.

Uganda is one of the world's leading coffee exporters, and coffee supports the livelihoods of more than 3.5 million people. Around 90% of the country's coffee is produced by smallholder farmers, most farming only a few acres of land. Yet many of these farmers remain among the least rewarded participants in the entire coffee value chain. We believe this is not because Ugandan coffee lacks quality.

It is because the people who grow it have been disconnected from the value it creates.

Our vision is to export premium single-origin Ugandan specialty coffee while transforming the lives of the farmers behind every bean.

Every bag of coffee will carry more than exceptional flavour. It will carry a name, a village, a family, and a story. Through full traceability, partners around the world will know exactly who grew their coffee. Cafés, churches, businesses, ethical buyers, and individual consumers will not simply buy coffee; they will become partners in changing lives. A percentage of every sale will return directly to the farmer who produced that coffee, ensuring that prosperity is shared throughout the value chain rather than concentrated at the very top.

Our long-term dream reaches far beyond exporting coffee. We want to build communities where farming once again becomes a source of dignity instead of survival, where young people choose opportunity over unemployment. Where mothers no longer sell their harvest out of desperation. Where every cup of Ugandan coffee tells a story of resilience, hope, and transformation.

The inspiration behind RAKI Coffee is captured in the Greek word Meraki: to do something with soul, passion, creativity, and love, leaving a part of yourself in your work. That is precisely what Uganda's coffee farmers do every single day. Every seed they plant, every tree they nurture, and every harvest they gather carries years of sacrifice, perseverance, and unwavering hope. Yet too often, the world enjoys the richness of their coffee without ever knowing the people whose hands made it possible. RAKI Coffee exists to restore that connection.

We are more than a coffee exporter. We are a vessel through which governments, development agencies, philanthropic foundations, impact investors, ethical businesses, churches, cafés, corporations, and individuals can become part of a story far greater than coffee itself. A story where fair trade becomes more than a principle, where business becomes a force for good, and where every purchase becomes an opportunity to invest in people.

We are committed to inclusive economic growth, sustainable agriculture, women's empowerment, youth employment, and fair trade; our invitation extends far beyond one institution. We welcome governments, international development partners, philanthropic organisations, ethical coffee buyers, impact investors, corporations, and partners across Europe, North America, Australia, Asia, and the wider global community to join us in building a more equitable coffee value chain.
