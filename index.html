Who We Are

Coffee with a Traceable Soul.

Our Vision

Our vision is to export premium single-origin Ugandan specialty coffee while transforming the lives of the farmers behind every bean.

Our Mission

We are committed to inclusive economic growth, sustainable agriculture, women's empowerment, youth employment, and fair trade — connecting the world to the people behind every cup.

What Happens
1

You Request a Harvest

Tell us your preferred region, roast profile, and quantity. Every custom harvest starts with your order.

2

We Match You With Outgrowers

We pair your request with the outgrower farms best suited to it — by region, altitude, and variety.

3

Cherries Are Hand-Picked

Farmers hand-pick ripe cherries specifically for your order — not from a general harvest pool.

4

Processed & Roasted to Order

Your beans are washed, dried, and roasted separately, keeping the batch traceable to your harvest alone.

5

Delivered With Its Story

Your custom harvest arrives with full traceability — which farm, which farmers, which week it was picked.

What We Stand For

Commitments We Don't Compromise On.

Full Traceability

Every bag carries a name, a village, a family. Know exactly who grew your coffee, from farm to cup.

Fair, Direct Pricing

A share of every sale returns directly to the farmer who grew it — no middlemen deciding what their harvest is worth.

Sustainable Farming

Shade-grown and ethically sourced — farmed to protect the land for the next harvest and the next generation.

Women's Empowerment

Our Women Coffee Program helps women farmers set fair terms instead of accepting whatever price they are offered.

Youth Development

From the farm to the roastery, we train the next generation of Ugandan coffee talent.

Community Partnership

500+ outgrower farmers, one shared purpose — building communities where coffee is a source of dignity.

Leadership

The people guiding RAKI's mission to connect the world to the farmers behind every cup.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

The Beginning

RAKI Coffee Founded

A small outgrower network in the hills of Bududa comes together around one belief: a coffee tree can educate a child.

Building the Network

Women Coffee Program Launched

Women farmers begin setting their own terms instead of accepting whatever price they are offered.

Growing Together

Custom Harvest Introduced

Buyers can now request a harvest picked and processed just for them — full traceability, start to finish.

Expanding the Trail

Meraki Trail Opens

Coffee agro-tourism begins — visitors walk the same terraces where RAKI's coffee is grown.

500 Strong

500+ Outgrower Farmers

The network grows to more than 500 outgrower farmers across Uganda's prime coffee regions.

Today

Brewing Hope, Cup by Cup

RAKI continues connecting the world to the people behind every cup — one harvest, one farmer, one story at a time.
