Outgrowers

Our Network.

500+ outgrower farmers across Uganda's prime coffee regions.

Mukono District

Central Region — 200+ partners

Our home base near Sezibwa Falls — the founding outgrower community and origin of RAKI's Meraki Trail.

Kapchorwa, Mt. Elgon

Eastern — High-altitude Arabica, 120+ farmers

High-altitude slopes of Mt. Elgon produce bright, complex Arabica — farmers here specialize in washed processing.

Bushenyi District

Western — Robusta specialists, 100+ farmers

Western Uganda's Robusta heartland, supplying the bold body behind our Sezibwa River Roast.

Jinja / Sezibwa Basin

Lake Victoria — 80+ farmers, river terraces

River-terrace farms along the Sezibwa basin, where coffee and water heritage meet.

Zirobwe, Luweero

Central North — 50+ new outgrowers

Our newest outgrower community, onboarded through the Farmer Development program.

Kasese, Rwenzori

Western Ranges — Rwenzori foothill Arabica, 40+ farmers

Our newest partnership in the Rwenzori foothills, expanding RAKI's outgrower network into Uganda's western highlands.
