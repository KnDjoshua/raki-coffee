FAQ

Common
Questions.

How does payment work?

There's no automatic charge when you submit the form. Our team contacts you within 24 hours to confirm your plan, then arranges payment directly with you — by Mobile Money (MTN/Airtel), bank transfer, or cash on delivery.

Where do you deliver?

Delivery is arranged directly with you based on your location when we confirm your order.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Nothing bills automatically in the first place — every renewal is arranged with you directly, so there's nothing to cancel until you choose to continue.

What grind options are available?

Whole bean, fine, medium, or coarse — you choose when you sign up, and can change it anytime you're in touch with us.

What are your hours, and where are you based?

We're based on Sezibwa Road, Mukono, Uganda, and open Mon-Sat: 7am-6pm.
