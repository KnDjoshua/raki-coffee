FAQ

Common
Questions.

How does booking and payment work?

There's no automatic charge when you submit a request. Our team contacts you within 24 hours to confirm details, then arranges payment directly with you — by Mobile Money (MTN/Airtel), bank transfer, or cash.

Can I book for a group?

Yes — just include your party size in the booking form. We'll confirm group details when we reach out.

How do I know which trail is right for me?

It depends on time and effort: Coffee Discovery is an easy half-day introduction, Coffee Agro Tourism is a moderate 3-day journey, and the Meraki Expedition is a challenging 4-day immersion. Each level's card lists exactly what's included.
